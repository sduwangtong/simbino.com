Tiny Doctor

中文名：三假小医院

A pretend doctor kit for kids ages 3–8, run by a grown-up with a wink.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

Point the pretend scanner at your child and the instrument finds something wonderfully silly — a sleepy ice-cream worm in the tummy, sugar bugs napping on a tooth, germs holding a sports day on an unwashed hand. The child presses one green button, the machine hums, and everything comes back clean. The lesson lands because the fix is theirs.

六台假仪器，专治不听话

假医生、假设备、假诊断。家长拿着手机当「扫描仪」，B超找出冰淇淋虫，显微镜照出手上的细菌。每一屏都清楚标着「模拟演示」，玩笑模拟不吓孩子，还附家长小抄。适合 3 到 8 岁，亲子一起玩。
