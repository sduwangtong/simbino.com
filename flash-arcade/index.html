Flash Arcade

About

Flash Arcade rebuilds the browser games of the 2000s for touch: six of them fully playable, plus an illustrated catalog of the ones we have not remade yet. Everything works offline, and there are no in-app purchases.

Download Flash Arcade on the App Store · Learn more: English overview · 中文介绍

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. There is no sign-up and no login. Open the app and play.
  • Does it work without internet? Yes. Every game is fully playable in airplane mode. Only the wish-list vote counter and the ads need a connection.
  • How do I switch between English and Chinese? Tap the button in the top-right corner of the header on the home screen.
  • Where did my saved game go? Progress is stored on your device only, so deleting the app deletes it. It does not sync between devices.
  • Can I ask for a game to be added? Yes — vote in the 许愿池 wish list on the home screen, or email us. The totals genuinely decide what we build next.
  • Why do I see ads? Ads are what keep every game free with no paywall. If you decline tracking when iOS asks, the app works exactly the same; the ads are just less relevant.
